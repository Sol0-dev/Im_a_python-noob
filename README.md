To complete the **Moore's Law** challenge, update line 5 to apply the exponential growth formula and then print the result on line 6.

### **How it works:**

This line does three small steps in order:

1. **`years / 2` $\rightarrow$ `10 / 2 = 5**`
* Since the count doubles **every 2 years**, we divide 10 years by 2 to figure out *how many times* it will double.
* In 10 years, it doubles **5 times**.


2. **`2 ** 5` $\rightarrow$ `2 * 2 * 2 * 2 * 2 = 32**`
* In Python, `**` means exponent ("to the power of").
* Doubling 5 times in a row means multiplying by $2$ five times, which equals **32**.


3. **`transistors * 32`**
* Finally, we take our starting $25\text{ billion}$ transistors and multiply by $32$.
* $25\text{ billion} \times 32 = \mathbf{800\text{ billion}}$ transistors in 10 years!
